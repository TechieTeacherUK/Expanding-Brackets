How do I type my answer?
  • Powers: type x^2 for x squared, or tap the ^ button and then 2.
  • Multiplying: write 3x or 3*x. Both work.
  • Brackets: 3(x + 2) works. Spaces don't matter.
  • The order of the terms doesn't matter, so 5 + 2x is fine for 2x + 5.
